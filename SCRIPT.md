# "What If Pax Silica Is Approved? (Philippines & Aeta Communities)" — Visual Status Short (No Voiceover)
Format: 60.0 s (1800 frames @ 30 fps, 1080x1920 -> 2K 1440x2560), 2D bean-head characters in a painted 3D toon world, pure visual storytelling + minimal upper status pills (no narration).

## Attributed Facts
- Pax Silica ("Silicon Peace") is a U.S.-led multilateral initiative launched in December 2025 to diversify and secure supply chains for semiconductors, AI hardware, and critical minerals; the Philippines signed in April 2026. (U.S. Department of State, U.S. Embassy, Philippine Board of Investments)
- Flagship proposal: a 4,000-acre (~1,620-hectare) Economic Security Zone and AI-Native Industrial Acceleration Hub in New Clark City, Capas, Tarlac, linking Subic Bay, Clark, Manila, and Batangas along the Luzon Economic Corridor. Formal framework agreements are targeted for late 2026. (BOI, Fulcrum, SCMP, GMA News, Philstar)
- Economic projections: proponents and planners project $40B-$70B+ in investments over decades and 100,000-200,000 high-tech jobs, shifting the Philippines from basic electronics assembly/testing to advanced semiconductor packaging and mineral processing. (Channel NewsAsia, Tech Policy Press, PwC, Fulcrum)
- Aeta Indigenous community concerns: Aeta residents and tribal leaders in Capas (including Barangay O'Donnell, Sitio Manabayukan, and Hungey leaders) and activist/environmental groups warn of ancestral land displacement, lack of prior consultation, water/power strain, and geopolitical entanglement in U.S.-China rivalry. (ABS-CBN News, Gulf News, Philstar, Inquirer, Tech Policy Press)
- Government & official assurances: BCDA, DTI, and Malacañang state that Philippine laws strictly govern the zone (no diplomatic immunity), dialogues run through the NCIP, facilities stay inside designated buildable zones sparing most Indigenous dwellings, clean-tech plants will not draw directly from local community water sources, and relocation assistance is promised if needed. (GMA News, Bombo Radyo, Philstar, PNA, PIA, Punto)
