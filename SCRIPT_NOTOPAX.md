# "No to Pax Silica: Aeta Ancestral Lands & Protest Perspectives" — Visual Short (No Voiceover)
Format: 60.0 s (1800 frames @ 30 fps, 1080x1920 -> 2K 1440x2560), 2D bean-head characters in a painted 3D toon world, balanced source-grounded visual storytelling.

## Attributed Sources
- **Aeta community & protest perspectives:** Aeta residents and tribal leaders in Capas, Tarlac (including Barangay O'Donnell, Sitio Manabayukan, and Hungey tribal leaders), farmers, student groups, and environmental coalitions report fears of ancestral land displacement, lack of prior consultation, eviction anxiety, water/power strain, and geopolitical entanglement, organizing "No to Pax Silica" / "Junk Pax Silica" rallies and petitions. (*ABS-CBN News, Gulf News, Philstar, Inquirer, Tech Policy Press, SCMP*)
- **Government & agency responses:** BCDA, DTI, and Malacañang maintain the 4,000-acre New Clark City site sits within state-owned development zones, operates strictly under Philippine law (with no diplomatic immunity), undergoes dialogues via the NCIP, restricts builds to designated zones sparing most Indigenous dwellings, and avoids drawing directly from local community water sources, with relocation support promised if needed. (*Philstar, GMA News, PNA, PIA, Punto, Bombo Radyo*)
